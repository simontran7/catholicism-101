# Mass

Jesus offers himself to the Father for us in the Eucharistic Sacrifice, and the Father gives us Christ in Holy Communion.

## Before Mass

- Enter the church quietly and prayerfully.
- (_Dip your right hand in the holy water font_) ✛.
- Enter a pew.
- Make a personal prayer.

> [!WARNING]
> During Mass, if you need to exit/re-enter your pew, bow from the waist toward the altar.

> [!NOTE]
> During Mass, follow the posture and gestures of the congregation and the directions of the local parish. The exact posture can vary somewhat by country, diocese, or local custom.

## Introductory Rites

### 1. Entrance Chant

(_Stand_)

_The priest, deacon, and ministers process into the church while the entrance chant/hymn is sung._

_The priest and deacon reverence the altar; the priest kisses the altar. Incense may be used._

Priest: ✛ In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.  
**People: Amen.**

### 2. Greeting

Priest: The Lord be with you.  
**People: And with your spirit.**

### 3. Penitential Act

Priest: Brothers and sisters, let us acknowledge our sins, and so prepare ourselves to celebrate the sacred mysteries.

**Everyone:**

> **I confess to almighty God  
> and to you, my brothers and sisters,  
> that I have greatly sinned,  
> in my thoughts and in my words,  
> in what I have done, and in what I have failed to do,  
> (_strike left breast_) through my fault,  
> (_strike left breast_) through my fault,  
> (_strike left breast_) through my most grievous fault,  
> therefore I ask blessed Mary ever-Virgin,  
> all the Angels and Saints,  
> and you, my brothers and sisters,  
> to pray for me to the Lord our God.**

Priest: May almighty God have mercy on us, forgive us our sins, and bring us to everlasting life.  
**People: Amen.**

### 4. Gloria (On Sundays outside Advent and Lent, and on solemnities and feasts)

(_stand_)

**Everyone:**

> **Glory to God in the highest,  
> and on earth, peace to people of good will.**
>
> **We praise you,  
> we bless you,  
> we adore you,  
> we glorify you,  
> we give you thanks for your great glory,  
> Lord God, heavenly King,  
> O God, almighty Father.**
>
> **Lord Jesus Christ, Only Begotten Son,  
> Lord God, Lamb of God,  
> Son of the Father,  
> you take away the sins of the world, have mercy on us;  
> you take away the sins of the world, receive our prayer;  
> you are seated at the right hand of the Father, have mercy on us.**
>
> **For you alone are the Holy One,  
> you alone are the Lord,  
> you alone are the Most High,  
> Jesus Christ,  
> with the Holy Spirit,  
> in the glory of God the Father.**
>
> **Amen.**

### 5. Collect

Priest: Let us pray.   
Priest: _prays the collect specific to the day._  
**People: Amen.**

## Liturgy of the Word

(_Sit_)

### 6. First Reading

Reader: _Reads the first reading._  
Reader: The word of the Lord.  
**People: Thanks be to God.**

### 7. Responsorial Psalm

_The cantor/reader proclaims the psalm, and the people respond with the response._

### 8. Second Reading (On Sundays and solemnities)

Reader: _Reads the second reading._  
Reader: The word of the Lord.    
**People: Thanks be to God.**

### 9. Gospel Acclamation (except during Lent)

(_Stand_)

_The Alleluia and its verse are sung._

### 10. Gospel Reading

Deacon/Priest: The Lord be with you.  
**People: And with your spirit.**

Deacon/Priest: A reading from the holy Gospel according to _N_.  
**People: (_Make a small cross with the thumb on the forehead, lips, and heart_) Glory to you, O Lord.**  
Deacon/Priest: _Reads the Gospel._  
Deacon/Priest: The Gospel of the Lord.    
**People: Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ.**  

### 11. Homily

(_Sit_)

Priest: _gives the homily._

### 12. Profession of Faith (On Sundays and solemnities)

(_Stand_)

**Everyone:**

> **I believe in one God,  
> the Father almighty,  
> maker of heaven and earth,  
> of all things visible and invisible.**
>
> **I believe in one Lord Jesus Christ,  
> the Only Begotten Son of God,  
> born of the Father before all ages.  
> God from God, Light from Light,  
> true God from true God,  
> begotten, not made, consubstantial with the Father;  
> through him all things were made.  
> For us men and for our salvation  
> he came down from heaven,**
>
> **(_bow_) and by the Holy Spirit was incarnate of the Virgin Mary,  
> and became man. (_end bow_)**
>
> **For our sake he was crucified under Pontius Pilate,  
> he suffered death and was buried,  
> and rose again on the third day  
> in accordance with the Scriptures.  
> He ascended into heaven  
> and is seated at the right hand of the Father.  
> He will come again in glory  
> to judge the living and the dead  
> and his kingdom will have no end.**
>
> **I believe in the Holy Spirit, the Lord, the giver of life,  
> who proceeds from the Father and the Son,  
> who with the Father and the Son is adored and glorified,  
> who has spoken through the prophets.**
>
> **I believe in one, holy, catholic and apostolic Church.  
> I confess one Baptism for the forgiveness of sins  
> and I look forward to the resurrection of the dead  
> and the life of the world to come.**
>
> **Amen.**

OR

**Everyone:**

> **I believe in God,  
> the Father almighty,  
> Creator of heaven and earth,**
>
> **and in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord,  
> (_bow_) who was conceived by the Holy Spirit,  
> born of the Virgin Mary (_end bow_),  
> suffered under Pontius Pilate,  
> was crucified, died and was buried;  
> he descended into hell;  
> on the third day he rose again from the dead;  
> he ascended into heaven,  
> and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty;  
> from there he will come to judge the living and the dead.**
>
> **I believe in the Holy Spirit,  
> the holy catholic Church,  
> the communion of saints,  
> the forgiveness of sins,  
> the resurrection of the body,  
> and life everlasting.**
>
> **Amen.**

### 13. Prayers of the Faithful

Priest: _Offers the intentions._  
**People: Lord, hear our prayer.**  
Priest: _Prays the concluding prayer._  
**People: Amen.**

## Liturgy of the Eucharist

### 14. Preparation of the Gifts

(_Sit_)

_The altar is prepared and the gifts of bread and wine are brought forward._

_An offertory chant/hymn may be sung._

Priest: Blessed are you, Lord God of all creation, for through your goodness we have received the bread we offer you: fruit of the earth and work of human hands, it will become for us the bread of life.  
**People: Blessed be God for ever.**

Priest: Blessed are you, Lord God of all creation, for through your goodness we have received the wine we offer you: fruit of the vine and work of human hands, it will become our spiritual drink.  
**People: Blessed be God for ever.**

> [!NOTE]
> If there is singing, these prayers may be said quietly by the priest. If there is no singing, they are said aloud and the people respond.

_The priest may wash his hands._

(_Stand_)

Priest: Pray, brothers and sisters, that my sacrifice and yours may be acceptable to God, the almighty Father.  
**People: May the Lord accept the sacrifice at your hands for the praise and glory of his name, for our good and the good of all his holy Church.**  

### 15. Prayer over the Offerings

Priest: _Prays the Prayer over the Offerings specific to the day_ ... Through Christ, our Lord.   
**People: Amen.**   

### 16. Eucharistic Prayer

Priest: The Lord be with you.  
**People: And with your spirit.**  
Priest: Lift up your hearts.    
**People: We lift them up to the Lord.**    
Priest: Let us give thanks to the Lord our God.    
**People: It is right and just.**

Priest: _Prays the Preface specific to the day_ ... And so, with the Angels and all the Saints we declare your glory, as with one voice we acclaim:

**Everyone:**

> **Holy, Holy, Holy Lord God of hosts.  
> Heaven and earth are full of your glory.  
> Hosanna in the highest.  
> Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord.  
> Hosanna in the highest.**

(_Kneel_)

Priest: You are indeed Holy, O Lord, the fount of all holiness. Make holy, therefore, these gifts, we pray, by sending down your Spirit upon them like the dewfall, so that they may become for us the Body and Blood of our Lord Jesus Christ.At the time he was betrayed and entered willingly into his Passion, he took bread and, giving thanks, broke it, and gave it to his disciples, saying: Take this, all of you, and eat of it, for this is my Body, which will be given up for you.

_The priest elevates the Host._

In a similar way, when supper was ended, he took the chalice and, once more giving thanks, he gave it to his disciples, saying:   Take this, all of you, and drink from it, for this is the chalice of my Blood, the Blood of the new and eternal covenant, which will be poured out for you and for many for the forgiveness of sins. Do this in memory of me.

_The priest elevates the Chalice._

Priest: The mystery of faith.

**People: We proclaim your Death, O Lord, and profess your Resurrection until you come again.**

(_Stand_)

Priest: Therefore, as we celebrate the memorial of his Death and Resurrection, we offer you, Lord, the Bread of life and the Chalice of salvation, giving thanks that you have held us worthy to be in your presence and minister to you. Humbly we pray that, partaking of the Body and Blood of Christ, we may be gathered into one by the Holy Spirit. Remember, Lord, your Church, spread throughout the world, and bring her to the fullness of charity, together with _the Pope_ and _our Bishop_ and all the clergy. Remember also our brothers and sisters who have fallen asleep in the hope of the resurrection, and all who have died in your mercy: welcome them into the light of your face. Have mercy on us all, we pray, that with the Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of God, with the blessed Apostles, and all the Saints who have pleased you throughout the ages, we may merit to be coheirs to eternal life, and may praise and glorify you through your Son, Jesus Christ. Through him, and with him, and in him, O God, almighty Father, in the unity of the Holy Spirit, all glory and honour is yours, for ever and ever.    
**People: Amen.**

## Communion Rite

### 17. The Lord's Prayer

Priest: At the Savior's command and formed by divine teaching, we dare to say:

**Everyone:**

> **Our Father, who art in heaven,  
> hallowed be thy name;  
> thy kingdom come,  
> thy will be done  
> on earth as it is in heaven.  
> Give us this day our daily bread,  
> and forgive us our trespasses,  
> as we forgive those who trespass  
> against us;  
> and lead us not into temptation,  
> but deliver us from evil.**

Priest: Deliver us, Lord, we pray, from every evil, graciously grant peace in our days, that, by the help of your mercy, we may be always free from sin and safe from all distress, as we await the blessed hope and the coming of our Savior, Jesus Christ.  
**People: For the kingdom, the power and the glory are yours, now and for ever.**

### 18. Sign of Peace

Priest: Lord Jesus Christ, who said to your Apostles: Peace I leave you, my peace I give you; look not on our sins, but on the faith of your Church, and graciously grant her peace and unity in accordance with your will. Who live and reign for ever and ever.  
**People: Amen.**  
Priest: The peace of the Lord be with you always.    
**People: And with your spirit.**  
Priest: Let us offer each other the sign of peace.  

(_Exchange a sign of peace with those nearby._)

### 19. Fraction Rite

_The priest breaks the consecrated Host while the following is sung or recited._

**Everyone: Lamb of God, you take away the sins of the world, have mercy on us. Lamb of God, you take away the sins of the world, have mercy on us. Lamb of God, you take away the sins of the world, grant us peace.**

_The priest privately prepares himself for Communion._

### 20. Holy Communion

Priest: Behold the Lamb of God, behold him who takes away the sins of the world. Blessed are those called to the supper of the Lamb.

**Everyone: Lord, I am not worthy that you should enter under my roof, but only say the word and my soul shall be healed.**

_The priest receives Holy Communion._

_The Communion chant/hymn begins._

(_Approach the minister to receive Holy Communion._)

(_When you are next in line, do a slight bow towards the Eucharist._)

Minister: The Body of Christ.    
**People: Amen.**

(_Return to your pew._)

(_Kneel_)

(_Pray in thanksgiving after receiving Holy Communion._)

(_Sit_)

### 21. Prayer after Communion

(_Stand_)

Priest: Let us pray.  
Priest: _Prays the Prayer after Communion._  
**People: Amen.**

## Concluding Rites

### 22. Announcements (If any)

(_Sit_)

Priest/announcer: _Gives any necessary announcements._

### 23. Final Blessing

(_Stand_)

Priest: The Lord be with you.    
**People: And with your spirit.**  
Priest: May almighty God bless you, ✛ the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit.    
**People: Amen.**

### 24. Dismissal

Priest: Go forth, the Mass is ended.  
**People: Thanks be to God.**  

_Closing hymn is sang._

_The ministers process out._

## After Mass

(_Kneel_)

- Make a personal prayer.
- Exit your pew.
- (_Dip your right hand in the holy water font_) ✛.
- Exit the Church.
