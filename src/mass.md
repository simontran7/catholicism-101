# Mass

Before/After Mass
When passing by the altar
Facing the most Blessed Sacrament: genuflect on the right knee toward the Most Blessed Sacrament.
Else: bow from the waist toward the altar.
When entering/exiting a pew
Facing the most Blessed Sacrament: genuflect on the right knee toward the Most Blessed Sacrament.
Else: bow from the waist toward the altar.
When passing by the tabernacle in a side chapel
Facing the most Blessed Sacrament: genuflect on the right knee toward the Most Blessed Sacrament.
Else: None


When passing by the altar: bow from the waist toward the altar.
When presented before the Most Blessed Sacrament: make a slight bow toward the Blessed Sacrament.
