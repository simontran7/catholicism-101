## The Chaplet of Divine Mercy

https://thedivinemercy.org/message/devotions/pray-the-chaplet

In 1934, while her spiritual director, Father Michael Sopoćko, was away in the Holy Land, a demon appearing as a bright angel. It convinced Sister Faustina that her writings were useless and false,  and to destroy her initial diary notebooks. 

Upon his return, she confessed what she had done, and Father Sopoćko recognized the vision as a demonic trick meant to stop her from spreading the message of Divine Mercy. 

As a penance and a corrective measure, he ordered her to rewrite everything from memory. 

This is why the first part of her surviving Diary lacks strict chronological ordering.
