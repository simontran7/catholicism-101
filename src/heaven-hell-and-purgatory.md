# Heaven, Hell & Purgatory

> If a kid breaks their neighbor's window, they can apologize and be forgiven (Confession) but there are still consequences; they have to replace the window (Purgatory). Indulgences are like the kid's parent (God) giving them some (partial) or all (plenary) of the money needed to pay off the window
