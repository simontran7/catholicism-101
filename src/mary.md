# Marian Dogmas

## Mother of God (Theotokos)

Mary is truly the mother of Jesus Christ, who is both fully God and fully man. This was defined at the Council of Ephesus in 431.

## Perpetual Virginity

Mary remained a virgin before, during, and after the birth of Jesus. This teaching was affirmed by early church councils including the Second Council of Constantinople in 553.

## Immaculate Conception

God preserved Mary from the stain of original sin from the very first instant of her conception. Pope Pius IX officially defined this dogma in 1854 through the papal bull _Ineffabilis Deus_.

## The Assumption

At the end of her earthly life, Mary was taken up body and soul into heavenly glory. Pope Pius XII solemnly defined this dogma in 1950 via the apostolic constitution _Munificentissimus Deus_.
