# Precepts

The Five Precepts are basic obligations that the Catholic Church asks of her faithful. They provide a minimum foundation for participating in the sacramental and moral life of the Church.

1. Go to Mass on Sundays and holy days of obligation.
2. Go to confession at least once a year.
3. Receive Holy Communion at least once a year during the Easter season.
4. Follow the days of penance, abstinence, and fasting
    - Days of Penance: Every friday
    - Days of Abstinence (i.e., avoid meat): Fridays throughout Lent, and Ash Wednesday
    - Days of Fasting (i.e., two small meals, and one regular sized meal that doesn't exceed the size of the two small meals): Ash Wednesday and Good Friday
5. Help provide for the material and financial needs of the church within your abilities.
